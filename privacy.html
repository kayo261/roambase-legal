Privacy Policy

Roambase (travel companion app) · Last updated: August 15, 2026

Roambase is built and operated by an independent developer operating as GetLostAdv. The short version: everything you put into Roambase stays on your own device. There is no account, no server, and no cloud. We never collect, transmit, sell, or share your information, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Roambase is offline-first. The things you enter — your trips, stops, waypoints, itineraries, bookings, notes, and any costs — are saved locally on your phone (an on-device database). This data is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting a trip or booking in the app removes it; uninstalling the app removes all of it from your device.

Permissions, and why

Roambase asks for a permission only at the moment you use a feature that needs it, and only for that purpose:

  • Location — used only when you tap “log where I am now”, to record your current spot as a stop. The coordinates are stored on your device. Roambase does not track your location in the background.
  • Photos — used only when you choose a screenshot to add a booking from a confirmation. The image is read on your device (on-device text recognition) to prefill the booking; the picture is not stored by Roambase and is not sent anywhere.
  • Calendar — used only when you tap “Add to calendar”. Roambase opens your phone’s own “Add Event” screen, prefilled from the booking, and you confirm it before anything is saved. Roambase only writes the event you approve and does not read your other calendar entries.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no profiles.
  • No servers or cloud storage — your data is not uploaded anywhere.
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not sell or share your data. There is nothing to sell — we never receive it.
  • No background location tracking.

Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it entirely: delete individual trips, stops, or bookings inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything. We keep no copies, because we never had any.

Children

Roambase is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone — it does not collect data from anyone at all.

Changes

If Roambase ever adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, an optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated first and the change will be opt-in and clearly labelled. The “last updated” date above reflects the current version.
